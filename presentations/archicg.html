Why ArchiCG?

Enterprise Architecture repositories contain far more knowledge than one diagram can expose. ArchiCG explores what becomes possible when architectural descriptions are treated as interactive compound graphs rather than only as collections of static views.

PRESERVEKeep repository knowledge

Import architectural descriptions and preserve identities rather than recreating disconnected analytical copies.

EXPLORENavigate dependencies

Use graph traversal, filtering, paths, neighborhoods and alternative representations to investigate complexity.

PROJECTChange the question

Produce stakeholder views, matrices, timelines and visual-mining projections without changing the underlying architectural knowledge.

From repositories to an analytical graph.

ArchiCG complements established modeling environments. Data can be extracted, aggregated and analyzed locally, then useful views can be exported back toward conventional repositories.

STANDARDIZED EA VIEWPOINT

ArchiMate as a primary architectural ground

ArchiCG imports ArchiMate descriptions and exploits their concepts and relationships for interactive graph analysis. Identifier-preserving exchange allows analyzed views to remain connected to conventional modeling environments.

ArchiCG ↔ BPMNSM

Architecture and process are complementary viewpoints. The interesting experiment is not conversion for its own sake, but controlled navigation between architectural intent and detailed behavioral realization.

ARCHICG / ARCHIMATE

Capability ↓ realized by Business Process ↓ supported by Application Service
BPMNSM / BPMN

Behavioral realization

Research question: Can a user navigate from an architectural concern in ArchiCG to its BPMN realization in BPMNSM, work in the appropriate semantic ground, and return while preserving identity, provenance and traceability?

Analysis can become executable knowledge.

Graph algorithms, filters, viewpoints and visualizations can be composed into repeatable architectural analyses, while recorded interactions can turn analytical results into dynamic architectural storytelling.

manual exploration ↓ repeatable graph analysis ↓ parameterized architectural algorithm ↓ stakeholder projection ↓ storytelling / publication ↓ governance decision

Enterprise value

The objective is not graph visualization for its own sake. It is to make complex dependencies intelligible enough to support architecture, transformation and interoperability decisions.
