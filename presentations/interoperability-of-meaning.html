STRATEGIC GOVERNANCE

From consensus radar to semantic governance

ASD SSG practice and the Strategic Standards Radar explore how a community agrees on a coherent standards landscape, records tracked/candidate/adopted positions, distinguishes external, monitored, locally developed and actively influenced standards, and progressively connects those decisions to enterprise context and operational interoperability.

BPMNSM is the BPMN laboratory.

It is deliberately not the universal repository. It goes deep into the process viewpoint while remaining connected to the wider interoperability landscape.

INHABITING BABEL

Responsible Meaning Engineering

Different communities legitimately construct different conceptual worlds. Responsible interoperability makes assumptions, translations, losses, uncertainty and authority visible. AI can help navigate Babel; it should not pretend Babel has disappeared.

The human capability remains central.

The Open People Factory manifesto adds an essential organizational dimension: growing technological complexity cannot be mastered by a single expert. Sustainable systems require complementary expertise and collective practices across Enterprise Architecture, Knowledge Management and Management of Data.

Interoperability is ultimately exercised by people and institutions that must understand enough of each other’s worlds to collaborate without erasing their differences.

Why enterprises should care

The business case is not semantic elegance. It is the ability to change, collaborate and automate with less hidden loss of intent.

Continuous Operational Interoperability requires more than connected systems. It requires a governed ability to navigate, relate and operationalize multiple representations of meaning across the complete lifecycle of networked enterprises.

One semantic object. Two intelligible readings.

The recurring engineering pattern is not a generic diagram. It is a coordinated visual grammar + structured data + explicit metamodel + processing model. People navigate a representation suited to the question; machines operate on typed objects, relations and constraints underneath.

Do not replace specialized visual languages. Preserve them, make their semantics explicit, and connect them through controlled mediation.
